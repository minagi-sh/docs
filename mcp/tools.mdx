The reference below is generated from the tool definitions the server registers, so names, parameters and descriptions are exactly what your agent sees. Install and configuration are in the [MCP Server Overview](/mcp/overview); the signing flow for wallets that keep their own keys is in [External wallet](/mcp/external-wallet).

## How tools answer

- Every tool returns either `{ data }` or `{ error, isError: true }`. Read `isError` before anything else.
- Money comes back in base units **and** formatted (`"2500000"` and `"$2.50"`); prices and percentages are numbers.
- Tools that move money in keypair mode sign and send by themselves. In external-signer mode they return `needsSigning` with the unsigned transaction (or typed data) and `confirmWith`, the tool to call once your wallet has signed.
- A 402 answer means the request is paid (see [Authentication](/guides/authentication#paid-requests)); the SDK inside the server pays and retries when the wallet holds USDC.

## Common workflows

### Session

1. `dim_login` (configured key), or `dim_request_auth_message` then `dim_complete_login` (external wallet).
2. `dim_set_username` the first time.
3. `dim_get_balances` for the unified balance; `dim_get_deposit_info` for the addresses to fund it.

### Perps: fund, open, close

1. `dim_get_perps_status`: are perps open to you, and the rules (leverages, minimums, fees).
2. `dim_enable_perps` once. On Hyperliquid your EVM key is your account and signs two approvals; with `DIM_EVM_PRIVATE_KEY` set this is one call, otherwise finish with `dim_complete_perps_enrollment`.
3. `dim_move_to_perps` from any balance (Relay routes it), then `dim_get_perps_move` until it is done.
4. `dim_list_perp_markets`, `dim_quote_perp`, `dim_open_perp` with a `comment` (it becomes the first post of the trade's diary).
5. `dim_get_perp_positions` while it is open; `dim_close_perp` to close; `dim_get_perp_trades_of` for the closed rounds.
6. `dim_withdraw_from_perps` sends USD back to your home network.

### Posting about trades

- `dim_create_post` with `perpPositionId` (a note on the trade's page), `perpCoin`, `tradeId` or `tokenMint`.
- `dim_list_posts` with `scope: "position"` reads a trade's diary; `dim_list_post_replies` and `dim_reply_to_post` are the thread under a post.

### Referrals

- `dim_get_referral_summary` gives your code, your link and what is pending; `dim_claim_referral_rewards` moves pending rewards to your wallet.
- Pass `DIM_REFERRAL_CODE` on first sign-up to credit whoever brought you.

## Funding

Moving money into and out of your perps balance, through Relay.

### dim_move_to_perps

Move money into your perps balance from any of your balances: USDC or SOL on Solana, USDC or ETH on Arbitrum or Base, USDC or MON on Monad (default: Solana USDC). The route (Relay) swaps and bridges in one step; its fees are passed through and DIM adds none; the minimum is $10. With your own keys the Solana move is signed and sent here; an EVM move (or any move in external-signer mode) returns needsSigning with the transactions to sign and confirmWith dim_submit_perps_move_signature. Follow it with dim_get_perps_move. Counts against the daily spend limit.

| Parameter        | Type                                          | Required | Description                                                                                           |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `amount`         | number                                        | no       | Amount in units of the asset (e.g. 20 for 20 USDC, 0.5 for 0.5 SOL)                                   |
| `amountUsd`      | number                                        | no       | USDC moves only, same as amount: USD to move in (e.g. 20 for $20). Kept for older callers             |
| `network`        | string: `solana`, `arbitrum`, `base`, `monad` | no       | Where the money is now (default solana)                                                               |
| `asset`          | string: `USDC`, `SOL`, `ETH`, `MON`           | no       | What you move (default USDC). Solana: USDC or SOL; Arbitrum and Base: USDC or ETH; Monad: USDC or MON |
| `idempotencyKey` | string                                        | no       | Optional: the same key never starts a second move (safe retries)                                      |

### dim_withdraw_from_perps

Move USD out of your perps balance to your home network (see dim_get_deposit_info, change it with dim_set_funding_home), arriving as USDC: Hyperliquid withdraws to your own Arbitrum address, then (unless home is Arbitrum) Relay carries it home. DIM signs every step. The venue charges $1 and Relay its route fee, both passed through; the minimum is $5 and the amount must fit in withdrawable. Follow it with dim_get_perps_move.

| Parameter        | Type   | Required | Description                                                      |
| ---------------- | ------ | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `amountUsd`      | number | yes      | Amount in USD to take out of the perps balance (e.g. 10 for $10) |
| `idempotencyKey` | string | no       | Optional: the same key never starts a second move (safe retries) |

### dim_quote_perps_move

Preview a move into or out of perps without starting it: what leaves and from where, what arrives, the fee (passed through, DIM adds none), the time it usually takes, the minimum, the steps, and reject (min-deposit, min-withdraw, insufficient, route-unavailable) with the reason.

| Parameter   | Type                                          | Required | Description                                                                       |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `direction` | string: `in`, `out`                           | yes      | "in" (into perps) or "out" (to your home network)                                 |
| `amount`    | number                                        | no       | In: amount in units of the asset (20 USDC, 0.5 SOL). Out: USD (same as amountUsd) |
| `amountUsd` | number                                        | no       | Out (or a USDC move in): amount in USD                                            |
| `network`   | string: `solana`, `arbitrum`, `base`, `monad` | no       | In only: where the money is now (default solana)                                  |
| `asset`     | string: `USDC`, `SOL`, `ETH`, `MON`           | no       | In only: what you move (default USDC)                                             |

### dim_get_perps_move

One move into or out of perps as it stands: state (signing, moving, done, refunded, resting, stuck, failed), what left and what arrives, fees, each step as a readable line with its transaction link, where the money rests when the move stopped part way, and canResume.

| Parameter    | Type   | Required | Description                                                      |
| ------------ | ------ | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `transferId` | string | yes      | The transferId from dim_move_to_perps or dim_withdraw_from_perps |

### dim_submit_perps_move_signature

External wallet: finish starting a move into perps after signing what dim_move_to_perps returned (needsSigning). Solana: pass signedTransaction (base64), or signature if your wallet also broadcast it. EVM: send every transaction in order and pass txHashes. Use transferId and legIndex from confirmWith.params.

| Parameter           | Type   | Required | Description                                                                                                |
| ------------------- | ------ | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `transferId`        | string | yes      | confirmWith.params.transferId                                                                              |
| `legIndex`          | number | no       | confirmWith.params.legIndex (default 0)                                                                    |
| `signedTransaction` | string | no       | Solana: the signed transaction, base64                                                                     |
| `signature`         | string | no       | Solana: the transaction signature, when your wallet signed and broadcast it                                |
| `txHashes`          | string | no       | EVM: the hashes of the transactions you sent, in order, comma-separated (approve first when there was one) |

### dim_resume_perps_move

Finish a move that stopped part way with your money resting in your own wallet (canResume in dim_get_perps_move, e.g. USDC that reached Arbitrum on the way home). Only the steps left are quoted again.

| Parameter    | Type   | Required | Description                             |
| ------------ | ------ | -------- | --------------------------------------- |
| `transferId` | string | yes      | The transferId of a move with canResume |

## Perps

Perpetual futures on Hyperliquid: markets, tickets, positions, trades and the ranking.

### dim_get_perps_status

Whether perpetual futures on Hyperliquid are available to you, and the rules the server serves. Returns enabled and disabledReason (flag, region or venue), venue ("hyperliquid": real orders on your own Hyperliquid account; "synthetic": a development server that simulates positions and balance on live prices, where nothing moves), offered leverages, minimum position size, minimum move in and withdrawal, the open/close fee (venue taker fee + DIM builder fee, in bps of the position size), the bridge fee, the flat withdrawal fee and the maintenance fraction. Call this before trading; never assume the numbers.

_No parameters._

### dim_list_perp_markets

Perp markets: the venue's highest-volume coins (BTC, ETH, SOL, kBONK and more) with the live mark, 24h change, max leverage, funding rate, open interest, 24h volume, DIM's category (crypto, meme, stock, index, commodity, fx, other) and the spot token it is the perp of (spotAssetId, null when there is none). Sort by volume or open interest, narrow by category or a search. Coins are case-sensitive and k-prefixed coins are 1,000 units per contract (kBONK = 1,000 BONK).

| Parameter  | Type                                                                   | Required | Description                                                |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| `sort`     | string: `volume`, `oi`                                                 | no       | "volume" (24h volume) or "oi" (open interest, the default) |
| `category` | string: `crypto`, `meme`, `stock`, `index`, `commodity`, `fx`, `other` | no       | Only this kind of market                                   |
| `search`   | string                                                                 | no       | Matches the coin, symbol or name (e.g. "bonk")             |

### dim_get_perps_account

Your perps balance: balance (free cash), marginUsed, withdrawable (what a new position or a withdrawal can use), unrealized and realized PnL, and whether perps are enabled on your account. Money in USDC minor units and formatted.

_No parameters._

### dim_get_perp_positions

Pass includeFills to also get your closed rounds (realized result, close fee, and why: closed, liquidated, take-profit, stop-loss); page back through your whole history with fillsBefore = the nextFillsBefore of the previous answer. coin narrows both to one market.

| Parameter      | Type    | Required | Description                                                                                          |
| -------------- | ------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `includeFills` | boolean | no       | Also return your recent closed positions (fills)                                                     |
| `coin`         | string  | no       | Only this market (any case: sol is SOL)                                                              |
| `fillsBefore`  | string  | no       | Fills closed before this time (ISO): pass nextFillsBefore from the previous answer for the next page |
| `fillsLimit`   | number  | no       | Fills per page, 1-100 (default 50)                                                                   |

### dim_get_perp_market_activity

What DIM's traders are doing on one perp market: each position with its trader, side, leverage, size, entry, take profit and stop loss (public on DIM), whether it is open, closed or liquidated, its PnL and what the trader wrote with it; the latest opens and closes; and how many longs and shorts are open. One user's closed perp rounds, newest first: coin, side, leverage, size, entry and exit price, realized result (after the close fee), the close fee and DIM's share of it, why it ended (closed, liquidated, take-profit, stop-loss) and when. | Parameter | Type   | Required | Description                                                                                            |

### dim_get_recent_perp_trades

The latest closed perp rounds across every DIM trader, newest first: who closed it (@username and user id), coin, side, leverage, size, margin, entry and exit price, realized result (after the close fee) and its percent of the margin, the close fee and DIM's share of it, why it ended (closed, liquidated, take-profit, stop-loss) and when. sort "best" ranks them by realized result and days keeps only the rounds closed in the last N days: the best trades of the week. Public; the app's "latest trades" ticker. One trader's history is dim_get_perp_trades_of; the ranking by period is dim_get_perp_leaderboard.

| Parameter | Type   | Required | Description                                                                |

Includes your own rank when signed in. Public; house accounts never rank.

| Parameter | Type   | Required | Description                           |

### dim_quote_perp

Price a perp ticket without opening it: the fill price (the mark), position size, margin, open fee (with its DIM builder / venue taker split), total cost, liquidation price, and reject (min-notional, max-leverage, insufficient, closed) with the reason when it would be refused.

| Parameter    | Type                    | Required | Description                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------ | ----------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `coin`       | string                  | yes      | A coin from dim_list_perp_markets, spelled exactly as listed (case-sensitive): BTC, ETH, SOL, kBONK (= 1,000 BONK per contract)                                |
| `side`       | string: `long`, `short` | yes      | "long" (profit when the price rises) or "short" (profit when it falls)                                                                                         |
| `sizeUsd`    | number                  | yes      | The margin in USD you put up from your perps balance. The position size (notional) is sizeUsd × leverage and must reach the minimum (see dim_get_perps_status) |

| `takeProfit` | number                  | no       | Optional price in USD that closes the position in profit (above the price for a long, below for a short)                                                       |
| `stopLoss`   | number                  | no       | Optional price in USD that closes the position to cap the loss (below the price for a long, above for a short)                                                 |

The margin and the open fee leave your perps balance (fund it with dim_move_to_perps). If the price reaches the liquidation price the whole margin is lost.

| Parameter    | Type                    | Required | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------ | ----------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `coin`       | string                  | yes      | A coin from dim_list_perp_markets, spelled exactly as listed (case-sensitive): BTC, ETH, SOL, kBONK (= 1,000 BONK per contract)                                                                                                |
| `side`       | string: `long`, `short` | yes      | "long" (profit when the price rises) or "short" (profit when it falls)                                                                                                                                                         |

## Notes

- Coins are the venue's names and are case-sensitive: `BTC`, `ETH`, `SOL`, `kBONK` (a `k` coin is 1,000 units per contract).
- `dim_send_usdc` and `dim_send_token` to a `@username` are free; to a raw address they are withdrawals with a flat $0.50 fee and a $1 minimum.
