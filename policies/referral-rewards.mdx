## How Rewards Are Created

Referral rewards are created automatically when a referred user pays a **trading fee** to DIM: the fee on a **token swap**, DIM's **builder fee on a perps fill**, or the fee on a **withdrawal** to an outside address. Each reward is created with a **PENDING** status and linked to the fee collection that triggered it (`source`: `SWAP`, `PERP` or `TRANSFER`). Every fee is valued in USDC when it is collected, so rewards are always paid in USDC whatever token was swapped or withdrawn.

Reward amounts are a share of **DIM's fee**, never of the trade itself, at the [referral rates](/guides/referrals#the-rules):

- **Level 1**: 30% of the fee the referred user paid
- **Level 2**: 3% of that fee
- **Level 3**: 2% of that fee

A share that rounds to less than one minor unit of USDC creates no reward. Fees DIM does not keep create no rewards: the venue's own taker fee, the route fees of a move into or out of perps, and the network fees DIM pays for sends.

## Claiming Rewards

Pending rewards can be claimed at any time:

- **MCP**: `dim_claim_referral_rewards`
- **SDK**: `await sdk.referrals.claimRewards()`
- **REST**: `POST /referrals/me/claim`

Claiming marks every pending reward **CLAIMED** and transfers their total in USDC from the platform's escrow wallet to your Solana wallet in a single transaction, through DIM's transaction queue. A claim that cannot be confirmed on chain within five minutes is reverted: its rewards return to **PENDING** and can be claimed again. One claim per ten seconds.

## Unclaimed Reward Expiration

DIM reserves the right to cancel (reclaim) unclaimed rewards after **2 years** from the reward creation date. A cancelled reward is marked **CANCELLED**.

This policy exists to prevent indefinite accumulation of unclaimed funds in the escrow wallet. We encourage users to claim rewards periodically.

**Cancelled rewards cannot be recovered.**

## Forward-Only Accrual

Referral rewards accrue from the moment a referral code is applied. If a user applies a referral code after trading, the fees paid before that do not generate retroactive referral rewards. Only fees paid afterwards produce rewards for the referrer.

A referral code can be applied once per account, and a referral is permanent.
