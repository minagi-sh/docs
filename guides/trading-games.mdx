## What is coming

DIM is adding **trading games**: short, skill-based contests played on the same live markets that power the token charts and the perps' marks ([market data](/guides/prices)). The prices a game settles on will be the ones every other DIM screen already shows, read from the same deterministic tick series, so a result can always be checked against the chart.

The rules, the formats and the money involved are not settled, and they will be written down in the docs before anything opens.

## What is available today

Nothing. There are no game endpoints, no SDK methods and no agent tools for trading games yet, and nothing in the API accepts a stake for one. Anything that claims otherwise is not DIM.

## Meanwhile

- Trade with leverage on live markets, long or short, with a public page for every trade: [Perps trading](/guides/perps).
- Say what you think about a token or a position, and follow the people whose calls you rate: [Posts](/guides/posts) and [Follows](/guides/follows).
- Watch who is making money and on what: [Trades and PnL](/guides/trades).

This page will change to the full guide when the first game ships.
